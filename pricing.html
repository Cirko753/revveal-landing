What you pay for

Creators found, and creators analysed.

The list is for shortlisting. The analyses are for deciding who you pay.

A creator found

A TikTok creator who matches your brand's category, sectors and country, ranked by fit.

  • Followers, engagement rate, average views, total likes
  • Category, and the hashtags they actually use
  • Filters and sorting on those numbers
  • Favourites board: saved, contacted, signed, declined
  • Link to their TikTok page, CSV or JSON export

A detailed analysis

One creator opened in full, read from their 35 latest posts in date order.

  • Sponsored vs organic gap, on views and on engagement
  • Fake-follower risk
  • Reliability score /100, against creators in the same category
  • Written insights: format, hashtags, momentum, real posting rhythm
  • Every recent post with its own numbers, sponsored-only filter

Analyses cover 70% of the creators found. Most creators are ruled out on the numbers the list already shows, so the analyses go to the ones still standing.

Pricing questions

Before you pay.

Is this really not a subscription?

No. You create a campaign, you pay for it once, and that is the end of it. No seat, no renewal, no minimum term. Disappear for six months and you pay nothing in the meantime.

Is VAT added on top?

No. Revveal is run by a French sole trader under the VAT franchise scheme (article 293 B of the French tax code): no VAT is charged, so the price shown is the price you pay.

If your business is in another EU country, reverse charge applies, which is why checkout asks for your VAT number.

Will I get an invoice?

Yes. An invoice in your company's name is emailed to you once the payment goes through. That is why checkout asks for your company name, address and registration or VAT number.

How do I pay?

By card, on a secure Mollie payment page. Your card details never reach us.

What if my niche cannot fill a plan?

You see it before you pay. The purchase window shows how many creators match your brand, and greys out any plan it cannot fill. Padding the list with off-topic accounts to reach a number would waste your analyses.

What does the free trial include?

One free campaign per project: 15 creators and 5 detailed analyses, without a card. Creators who already publish sponsored posts come first, so the analyses have something to measure. Up to three free trials per person over 30 days.

Can I run several campaigns?

Yes. A project is one brand; each project holds up to five campaigns, and an account up to three projects. Each campaign is paid on its own.
