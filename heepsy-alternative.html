Revveal vs Heepsy

A Heepsy alternative for TikTok beauty campaigns.

Heepsy is a solid influencer search engine across four networks. Revveal does one narrower job: TikTok creators for beauty brands, and what their audience does when a post is paid. Here is how the two differ, and when Heepsy remains the better choice.

01In short

  • Heepsy is a subscription search engine for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn, with a free plan, and outreach and campaign tracking built in.
  • Revveal covers TikTok only, is built for beauty brands (France first), and is paid once per campaign. Each analysis compares a creator's sponsored posts with their organic ones.

If you run influencer marketing every month, on several networks, Heepsy probably suits you better. If you prepare a TikTok beauty campaign from time to time and want to know, before signing, which creators still perform once the post is paid, Revveal was built for that.

03What a Revveal analysis measures

A creator's engagement rate mixes their organic posts and their sponsored ones. On beauty TikTok, the two often have little in common. Revveal reads a creator's latest posts, not their most popular ones, spots those that are likely sponsored, and compares them with the creator's organic posts over the same period.

An example from our measurements: a French skincare account with about 380k followers gets 14.6% engagement on organic posts and 8.2% on sponsored ones. Its median views drop from 99k to 45k. Its overall engagement rate shows none of it.

Across the French beauty creators we measured, engagement drops by 46% at the median on a paid post, yet nearly one in three does as well as on organic posts. Telling them apart is the point of the analysis.

Other tools can show paid post performance too. On Revveal, it is the heart of every analysis. The methodology explains the calculation and its limits.

04What it costs

The two tools are not priced the same way, so the answer depends on how often you run campaigns.

One campaign from time to time

Revveal's Growth plan costs €89, once: 600 creators matched to your brand and 420 detailed analyses. Heepsy's Starter plan, taken for a single month, costs $89: 5,000 search results and 100 advanced profiles, then you cancel.

Every month

Heepsy's Starter plan billed yearly comes to $69 a month, $828 a year. If you search for creators every month, a subscription is likely to cost you less than buying a Revveal campaign each time.

A Heepsy “advanced profile” and a Revveal “analysis” are not the same unit. Compare what each one contains, not just the count.

05When Heepsy fits better

  • You also work on Instagram, YouTube or LinkedIn.
  • You search for creators every month.
  • You want outreach and campaign tracking in the same tool.
  • You want a free plan you can keep using.
  • Your niche is outside beauty, or your market outside France.

06When Revveal fits better

  • TikTok is your channel and beauty your category.
  • You want to know, before signing, who still performs once a post is paid.
  • You run campaigns from time to time and don't want a subscription in between.
  • You want a ranked list of creators for your niche, delivered as soon as the campaign is created.

07Getting started

There is nothing to migrate. You describe your brand (its category, a few keywords, its country) and Revveal delivers the matching creators. The free trial gives you 15 creators and 5 detailed analyses, with no card, to judge on your own niche.

08Questions

Is Revveal free like Heepsy?

Revveal has no free plan. It has a free trial: 15 creators and 5 detailed analyses, with no card. For ongoing free use, Heepsy's free plan is more generous.

Does Revveal cover Instagram or YouTube?

No. Revveal only covers TikTok.

Is there a subscription?

No. Each campaign is paid once, by card. There is nothing to cancel.

Which countries and niches does Revveal cover?

Revveal is built for beauty brands, and its database is deepest for French beauty creators. Before you pay, the purchase window only offers the plans your niche can actually fill.
