How to use it

Three minutes on the creator's profile.

  1. Spot their sponsored posts

    Look for TikTok's “Paid partnership” label, or #ad, #sponsored, #collab or a discount code in the caption. In France, paid posts must also carry “Publicité” or “Collaboration commerciale”.

  2. Note each post's numbers

    Views show on the profile thumbnail. Likes, comments, saves and shares sit in the icon column to the right of the video. “12.3K” can be typed as is.

  3. Add organic posts from the same period

    Pick unsponsored posts published around the same dates. The result appears as soon as there are two posts on each side.

Questions

How the calculation works.

What is the sponsored gap?

It is the difference between what a creator's sponsored posts do and what their usual posts do. An overall engagement rate mixes every post, and the best ones pull it up. What matters to a brand is what happens when the post is paid.

How is the result calculated?

Two measures, compared between sponsored and organic posts: views per post, as a median, and engagement per post (likes, comments, shares and saves divided by views), as an average. The percentage shown is the average of the two ratios: a brand buys reach and interaction, and keeping one while losing the other is not holding up.

Above 85%, sponsored posts hold up; between 60 and 85%, the drop is moderate; below 60%, it is sharp. These are the same definitions and thresholds as in the Revveal app.

Why the median for views?

Because a single post that went viral, on either side, would otherwise be enough to flip the verdict. The median keeps the typical post and ignores the exception.

How many posts should I enter?

Two on each side at minimum, three to five preferably. The more posts, the sturdier the result: on two posts, one bad day is enough to skew the figure. In the app, the analysis reads the creator's 35 most recent posts.

What does the 46% benchmark mean?

In October 2026, Revveal measured the gap of 22 French beauty creators who publish sponsored posts: at the median, their engagement drops by 46% when the post is sponsored, and 7 out of 22 do as well or better. It is a benchmark for beauty in France, on a small sample, not a norm for every niche.

Are my numbers sent anywhere?

No. The calculation runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is transmitted or stored, and there is no sign-up.
