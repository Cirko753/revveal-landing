This Privacy Policy explains how Arthur Werts, a sole trader registered in Boulogne-Billancourt, France ("we", "us"), handles personal data through Revveal (revveal.net). We are the data controller for the processing described here.

01Who this covers

This policy covers three different groups of people, and their situations are not the same:

  • Waitlist subscribers — you gave us an email address and nothing else.
  • Users — you have an account and run campaigns.
  • Creators — your public TikTok data appears in our creator database. You are not our user, you have no account with us, and you never agreed to our Terms. Section 11 is written for you.

02The waitlist

If you join the waitlist, we collect your email address, and nothing else — no name, no company, no tracking profile.

  • It is used to tell you when Revveal opens, and for occasional messages directly about that launch.
  • It is not sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or used for any unrelated mailing.
  • It is deleted on request, immediately, with no questions: reply to any message or write to contact@revveal.net.
  • If you have not created an account within twelve months of the launch notice, the address is deleted.

03Data we collect about you (the user)

  • Account data — your email address and password. The password is stored hashed by our authentication provider, Supabase; we never see it in plain text.
  • Project data — the company name, website, sectors, country, language and category you enter when creating a project, used to match relevant creators.
  • Campaign and favourites data — the campaigns you run and the creators you mark as saved, contacted, signed or declined, so you can track your own outreach.
  • Payment data — handled entirely by Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider and merchant of record. We receive confirmation that a payment succeeded and which plan was bought; we never receive your card details.

04Data we collect about creators

To power creator search, we maintain a database built from publicly available TikTok profile and post information, collected on our behalf by a third-party data provider (Bright Data). It consists of:

  • Username, display name, profile picture, biography, country and language;
  • Public counts — followers, likes, views — and statistics derived from them;
  • Recent public posts: captions, hashtags, engagement figures and thumbnail images;
  • Scores we compute ourselves from the above — engagement rate, audience quality, fake-follower risk, a reliability score, and a comparison between sponsored and organic posts.

We do not collect private messages, private or restricted accounts, contact details such as email addresses or phone numbers, or anything behind a login. We do not attempt to identify creators beyond what their public TikTok profile already shows.

To be explicit about a claim this site used to make and no longer does: this data is not obtained through the TikTok Research API. It is public profile and post information collected by our data provider.

05Legal basis

  • Waitlist — your consent, given by submitting the form. Withdrawable at any time.
  • Account and campaign data — performance of our contract with you (the Terms of Service), and our legal obligations for invoicing and accounting.
  • Creator data — our legitimate interest, and that of our users, in enabling creator discovery from information the creator has already published publicly on TikTok. We have weighed that against creators' interests, which is why the database is limited to public professional-facing information, why it holds no contact details, and why Section 11 gives an unconditional removal route.

06Retention

  • Waitlist addresses — until you ask for removal, or twelve months after the launch notice.
  • Account, project and campaign data — for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account deletes them.
  • Invoices — retained by Lemon Squeezy and by us for the period French accounting law requires, which overrides a deletion request for those records alone.
  • Creator records — kept in a shared pool and refreshed periodically as campaigns re-encounter them. To be precise rather than flattering: a record is not deleted merely because it has aged. Freshness governs whether a creator is re-checked before being added to a new campaign, not whether the record still exists. Creators are removed on request, under Section 11.

07Exported data

When a user exports campaign results as CSV or JSON, the file contains creator data as described in Section 4, plus a link to each creator's public TikTok profile. That export leaves our systems once downloaded: we do not track what happens to it, and the user becomes responsible for it under Section 7 of the Terms of Service.

09Cookies and analytics

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. There is nothing here to consent to, which is why you are not being asked. The only third party your browser contacts on these pages is Google Fonts, to load two typefaces.

Inside the application, a session token is stored in your browser so that you stay signed in between visits. It is strictly necessary for the Service to function and is not used to track you across other sites. If we ever add product analytics, this section will be updated before, not after.

10Your rights as a user

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your personal data, correct it, erase it, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a portable format. You may also withdraw consent where processing rests on consent.

  • You can delete your account yourself from Settings. Doing so removes your account, projects, campaigns and favourites.
  • For anything else, write to contact@revveal.net. We answer within one month.
  • If you are unhappy with our answer, you may complain to your national supervisory authority — in France, the CNIL.

11Your rights as a creator

If your public TikTok data appears in our creator database and you want it removed, you do not have to justify the request.

Email contact@revveal.net from an address, or with any proof, that lets us verify the account is yours. We delete your record from the database and prevent it from being collected again.

One honest limit: files that users already exported before your request are outside our systems, and removing you from our database cannot retroactively delete those copies. Under Section 6 of the Terms, those users remain responsible for the data they hold.

You have the same GDPR rights as anyone else in the database — access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection — exercised through the same address.

12Security

Data is stored with Supabase in the European Union and protected by row-level access controls, so each user can only read their own projects, campaigns and favourites. Passwords are hashed and never visible to us. All traffic is encrypted in transit. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we do not claim absolute security.

13International transfers

Our database is hosted in the European Union. Some sub-processors listed in Section 8 process data outside the EU — notably Lemon Squeezy in the United States. Where that happens we rely on the appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, offered by those providers.

14Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If a change materially affects how we handle data about you and we hold your email address, we will tell you directly.
