This Privacy Policy explains how Arthur Werts, a sole trader (entreprise individuelle) established at 13 rue de Clamart, 92100 Boulogne-Billancourt, France ("we", "us"), registered under SIREN 106 106 206, handles personal data through Revveal (revveal.net). We are the data controller for the processing described here, and you can reach us at contact@revveal.net.

01Who this covers

This policy covers four different groups of people, and their situations are not the same:

  • Visitors — you browse this site. We collect nothing that identifies you: no cookies, only anonymous visit counts (Sections 2 and 9).
  • Users — you have an account and run campaigns.
  • Creators — your public TikTok data appears in our creator database. You are not our user, you have no account with us, and you never agreed to our Terms. Section 11 is written for you.
  • Businesses we contact — you received an email from us presenting Revveal, at an address your business publishes. Section 12 is written for you.

02Visitors to this site

Browsing revveal.net collects nothing that identifies you. There is no form on these pages and no cookie; we only count visits anonymously, without tracking you (Section 9). You become identifiable to us only if you write to us, or if you create an account on the application, which is a separate address (app.revveal.net) covered by Section 3.

If you email us, we keep that message and your address for as long as it takes to answer you and to keep a record of the exchange, on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering enquiries.

03Data we collect about you (the user)

  • Account data — your email address and password. The password is stored hashed by our authentication provider, Supabase; we never see it in plain text.
  • Project data — the company name, website, sectors, country, language and category you enter when creating a project, used to match relevant creators.
  • Campaign and favourites data — the campaigns you run and the creators you mark as saved, contacted, signed or declined, so you can track your own outreach.
  • Free-plan usage — a record of your free trials (which project and campaign, and when), at most three entries, older ones being reused. It is kept even if you delete that campaign or project, so the free-plan limits — one trial per project, three per 30 days — can be enforced. If you delete your account, the record is detached from it and only an irreversible fingerprint (hash) of your email address remains, so that signing up again with the same address does not reset those limits.
  • Getting-started emails — after you sign up, we send you at most four short emails to help you start: a welcome, a reminder if you have not started your free trial or opened an analysis, and, once you have used your trial, one email presenting the paid plans your niche allows. We note on your account which of them were sent and whether you unsubscribed. Every one of these emails has an unsubscribe link, which does not stop the emails your account needs (sign-up confirmation, password reset, invoices).
  • Payment data — card details are handled entirely by Mollie B.V. (Netherlands), our payment provider, and never reach us. We remain the seller, so we do hold what an invoice requires: your billing name, address and company or VAT number, which you enter at checkout, plus the amount and the plan bought. Those details are passed to Mollie to issue the invoice and are not stored in our own database.

04Data we collect about creators

To power creator search, we maintain a database built from publicly available TikTok profile and post information, collected on our behalf by a third-party data provider (Bright Data). It consists of:

  • Username, display name, profile picture, biography, country and language;
  • Public counts — followers, likes, views — and statistics derived from them;
  • Recent public posts: captions, hashtags, engagement figures and thumbnail images;
  • Scores we compute ourselves from the above — engagement rate, audience quality, fake-follower risk, a reliability score, and a comparison between sponsored and organic posts.

We do not collect private messages, private or restricted accounts, or anything behind a login. A creator's biography is kept as they published it: when it contains an email address, we show that address on the creator's page so businesses can contact them. We do not look for contact details anywhere else, and we do not attempt to identify creators beyond what their public TikTok profile already shows.

To be explicit about a claim this site used to make and no longer does: this data is not obtained through the TikTok Research API. It is public profile and post information collected by our data provider.

05Legal basis

  • Account and campaign data — performance of our contract with you (the Terms of Service), and our legal obligations for invoicing and accounting.
  • Free-plan record — our legitimate interest in preventing abuse of the free plan.
  • Getting-started emails — our legitimate interest in helping you use the service you signed up for and, once you have tried it, in telling you about its paid plans. You can unsubscribe at any time, in one click.
  • Creator data — our legitimate interest, and that of our users, in enabling creator discovery from information the creator has already published publicly on TikTok. We have weighed that against creators' interests, which is why the database is limited to public professional-facing information, why the only contact detail it shows is an email address the creator published in their own bio, and why Section 11 gives an unconditional removal route.
  • Business prospecting — our legitimate interest in presenting Revveal to businesses for which it is relevant. We only write to addresses that businesses publish for professional enquiries, about a subject related to their activity, and every email offers a one-word way out (Section 12).

06Retention

  • Account, project and campaign data — for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account deletes them, including the record of the getting-started emails sent to you and of your unsubscribe.
  • Free-plan record — at most three entries while your account exists. Once it is deleted, only the fingerprint of your email address and the trial dates remain, and they are erased 30 days after the trial they relate to.
  • Invoices — held by Mollie on our behalf and retained for the period French accounting law requires (10 years), which overrides a deletion request for those records alone.
  • Creator records — kept in a shared pool and refreshed from time to time from the same public sources. To be precise rather than flattering: a record is not deleted merely because it has aged; an older record simply carries older statistics until its next update. Creators are removed on request, under Section 11.
  • Prospecting data — at most three years after our last exchange. An address that asked us to stop is kept on the do-not-contact list alone, with nothing else, so that we never write to it again.

07Exported data

When a user exports campaign results, or the detailed analysis of a creator they have unlocked, as CSV or JSON, the file contains creator data as described in Section 4, plus a link to each creator's public TikTok profile. An unlocked analysis adds the statistics of the creator's recent public posts (links, captions, hashtags, views, likes, comments, shares and saves), the figures computed from them, and any contact email the creator published in their bio. That export leaves our systems once downloaded: we do not track what happens to it, and the user becomes responsible for it under Section 7 of the Terms of Service, which limit it to assessing and contacting creators about the user's own collaborations and forbid publishing or reselling it.

09Cookies and analytics

This website sets no cookies and runs no advertising scripts. To know how many people visit it, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, an audience measurement tool that sets no cookie, stores nothing in your browser, does not use your IP address to identify you and does not follow you across other sites. It only gives us aggregate figures: pages viewed, referring sites, country and type of browser. There is therefore nothing here to consent to, which is why you are not being asked. The third parties your browser contacts on these pages are Google Fonts, to load two typefaces, and Cloudflare, for this measurement.

Inside the application, a session token is stored in your browser so that you stay signed in between visits. It is strictly necessary for the Service to function and is not used to track you across other sites. If we ever add product analytics, this section will be updated before, not after.

10Your rights as a user

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your personal data, correct it, erase it, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a portable format. You may also withdraw consent where processing rests on consent.

  • You can delete your account yourself from Settings. Doing so removes your account, projects, campaigns and favourites.
  • For anything else, write to contact@revveal.net. We answer within one month.
  • If you are unhappy with our answer, you may complain to your national supervisory authority — in France, the CNIL.

11Your rights as a creator

If your public TikTok data appears in our creator database and you want it removed, you do not have to justify the request.

Email contact@revveal.net from an address, or with any proof, that lets us verify the account is yours. We delete your record from the database and prevent it from being collected again.

One honest limit: files that users already exported before your request are outside our systems, and removing you from our database cannot retroactively delete those copies. Under Section 6 of the Terms, those users remain responsible for the data they hold.

You have the same GDPR rights as anyone else in the database — access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection — exercised through the same address.

12Businesses we contact by email

We sometimes email businesses — mostly beauty brands — to present Revveal. This section is written for the people who receive those emails.

  • What we use — the professional email address the business publishes itself (on its website or legal notice: contact, partnerships, customer service…), with the page where we found it; the brand's name and website; and public information about its presence on TikTok, such as the number of public sponsored posts by creators that mention it. We do not buy lists, and we never guess or look up personal addresses.
  • What we record — the emails we send and when, and whether you replied, asked us to stop, or the address bounced. Our emails are plain text, with no tracking pixel and no tracked link: we do not know whether you opened them.
  • How — from a dedicated address, arthur@getrevveal.com, signed "Arthur, from Revveal". At most three emails, and they stop as soon as you reply, whatever your reply says.
  • Where — the list and the sending history are kept on our own equipment in France. The emails themselves pass through our mailbox provider, Google (Section 8).

To stop: reply "stop", or anything asking us to stop. Your address goes onto a do-not-contact list and we never write to it again. That list holds the address alone; we keep it precisely so that your request keeps being honoured. For access, correction or erasure, write to contact@revveal.net.

13Security

Data is stored with Supabase in the European Union and protected by row-level access controls, so each user can only read their own projects, campaigns and favourites. Passwords are hashed and never visible to us. All traffic is encrypted in transit. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we do not claim absolute security.

14International transfers

Our database, our payment provider and our image storage are all in the European Union. Some sub-processors listed in Section 8 do process data outside the EU — notably Bright Data, which collects public creator information for us, Google, through which our prospecting emails pass, and Mailtrap, which sends the emails of the app. Where that happens we rely on the appropriate safeguards offered by those providers, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, under which Mailtrap is certified.

15Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If a change materially affects how we handle data about you and we hold your email address, we will tell you directly.
