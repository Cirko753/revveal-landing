The blind spot

Every platform grades creators on their best unpaid work.

The engagement rate you are shown is almost always computed on a creator's most popular posts — a showcase, ranked by performance, sometimes spanning two years. Nothing in it was sponsored.

So you compare creators on the exact content that will never run for you. Then the brief lands, the post goes out with a partnership label, and the audience behaves differently.

Revveal reads the 35 most recent posts, in chronological order, separates the paid ones from the organic ones, and shows you the difference.

Straight answers

The questions worth asking before you trust a number.

How certain is the sponsored detection?

Not fully certain, and we say so in the product. No TikTok data source exposes a paid-partnership flag, so sponsored posts are identified from the caption — disclosure tags, partnership wording, brand mentions. Posts we are not sure about are labelled likely sponsored rather than counted silently.

It is the honest ceiling of the data available. We would rather show you the limit than a confident number built on one.

Where does the creator data come from?

From public TikTok profile and post information, collected through a third-party data provider. Usernames, bios, follower counts, post statistics and public thumbnails — nothing private, no messages, no email addresses, no private accounts.

Is this really not a subscription?

It is not. You create a campaign, you pay once for it, and that is the end of the transaction. There is no seat, no renewal and no minimum term. If you disappear for six months, you are charged nothing in the meantime.

What happens if my niche runs out of creators?

The campaign ends and tells you so plainly, with however many creators it genuinely found. Some niches in some countries simply do not hold a thousand relevant TikTok creators, and padding the list with off-topic accounts to hit a number would waste your analyses instead of your patience.

Can I get in touch with the creators through Revveal?

No. Revveal gives you the profiles, the analysis and a link to each creator's public TikTok page; outreach happens on your side, and the favourites board is there to track it — saved, contacted, signed, declined.

I am a creator. Can I be removed?

Yes. Email contact@revveal.net from an address that lets us verify the account is yours, and your record is deleted from the database. See the Privacy Policy for the detail.
